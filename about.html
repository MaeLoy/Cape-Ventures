About Cape Ventures

Established in 2022, Cape Ventures is a tourism company based in Cape Town, South Africa. The company was created to provide visitors with memorable experiences while exploring the city's attractions, culture and natural beauty.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide visitors with enjoyable and memorable Cape Town experiences through carefully planned tours and friendly service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted tourism company that connects visitors with the beauty, history and culture of Cape Town.
